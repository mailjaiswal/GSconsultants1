About GS Consultants

Your Trusted Professional Partner

GS Consultants is a professional CA, CS & Legal Services firm offering comprehensive solutions across accounting, taxation, audit, corporate compliance and legal services.

We work with individuals, startups, entrepreneurs, companies and growing businesses, helping them navigate financial and regulatory requirements at every stage of their journey.

Our services cover the complete business lifecycle — from incorporation and business setup to accounting, taxation, GST, audit, ROC compliance, company law, trademark registration and legal documentation.

We believe professional services should go beyond completing filings and meeting deadlines. Our role is to understand our clients’ requirements, provide clear and practical advice, and support them in making informed business decisions.
